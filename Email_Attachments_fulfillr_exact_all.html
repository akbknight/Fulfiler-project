How it works

Create your profile

Sellers and fulfillers add location, capacity, handling tags (e.g., luxury, food-grade), schedules, and preferences.

Match & choose

Our AI filters by proximity, capacity, trust score, and handling fit. See best, fastest, or lowest-cost options and confirm.

Fulfill & track

Orders flow automatically. Labels, pickup, and updates sync in real time. Both sides rate performance to improve future matches.

FAQ

What makes Fulfillr different?

A single platform that scales from peer-to-peer to enterprise hubs, with AI matching based on proximity, capacity, and specialized handling.

How do you ensure trust?

Verification, virtual tours, performance history, and two-way ratings feed a reliability score used in matching.

Who can host space?

Vetted home-based fulfillers, shared warehouses with spare capacity, and large hubs that meet compliance requirements.
